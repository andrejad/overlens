# What users see and can do

## Roadmaps, teams and workstreams

- **REQ_FE_001** — The roadmap's title is large and can be renamed by clicking it (Enter or clicking away saves, Esc cancels).
- **REQ_FE_002** *(withdrawn — decommissioned 2026-10-11: one roadmap per team, so there is no roadmap chooser, REQ_DAT_032)*
- **REQ_FE_007** *(withdrawn — archived roadmaps are fully editable, with the same edit affordances as the active one)*
- **REQ_FE_003** *(withdrawn — decommissioned 2026-10-11: see REQ_FE_002)*
- **REQ_FE_004** — The admin screen has a Teams section: add a team (name, a suggested tag, a color), rename, re-tag and recolor in place, and delete. In every row, the add-team row included, the name box, the tag box, the default colour, the roadmap items count and Delete sit on one line with the same height and baseline. Delete is confirmed in a dialog that names the team.

Deleting one always moves its deliverables and milestones to another workstream the user picks — there's no option to delete them — and the roadmap's last workstream can't be deleted (its delete is disabled with the reason).

## Keeping the layout honest

- **REQ_FE_010** — *Hard rule.* Two roadmap items in the same workstream row never overlap in time — on every roadmap screen and at every zoom level. The server refuses any write that would break this (REQ_API_051). *(Decision 2026-10-11, withdrawn 2026-10-17: a milestone no longer has a separate status icon that may cover a neighbour; its status is the diamond itself, REQ_FE_040. The diamond is part of the item and the no-overlap checks count it. Its name is cut to the free space of its own row and its date is left out where there is not room (REQ_FE_040), so nothing is drawn over a neighbour.)*
- **REQ_FE_011** — *Hard rule.* Rows are sticky: when the layout is recalculated, every roadmap item stays in the row it is in whenever it still fits there. The browser never saves a row of its own accord — a row is only saved by a user's drag or edit.
- **REQ_FE_012** — *Hard rule.* Dragging an edge or a whole bar stops when it reaches the neighbouring roadmap item in the same row, like hitting a wall.
- **REQ_FE_013** — *Hard rule.* A roadmap item only changes row when the user deliberately drags it to another row or workstream. Nothing else moves as a side effect.
- **REQ_FE_014** — *Hard rule.* On a team roadmap (the screen where layout is edited, REQ_FE_096) you can drag a roadmap item into any row of any shown workstream (REQ_FE_127), including a new row below the last. The target slot is outlined in the accent color when the drop is allowed and in red when it isn't.
- **REQ_FE_015** — Dropping onto an occupied slot moves nothing and says so.
- **REQ_FE_016** — Dropping into another workstream takes the deliverable out of the workstream it came from and puts it in the new one (any other workstreams it belongs to stay). A row that ends up empty closes up.
- **REQ_FE_017** — *Hard rule.* Anything switched off — copies, arrows, badges — is really gone from the screen (not just flagged hidden), and hidden copies give their rows back.

## The side panel and editing

- **REQ_FE_020** — Clicking a deliverable opens the side panel on the right; hovering it shows a three-dot button that opens the edit window.
- **REQ_FE_021** — The panel says which team the deliverable belongs to in its Team row (under Responsible), in both view and edit mode. Admins see a "(move)" link after the team name that opens the move dialog; there is no separate team line or button.
- **REQ_FE_032** — In the panel, a roadmap item's links are shown under its description, in both view and edit mode.
- **REQ_FE_022** — The panel can be dragged between 280 and 720px wide, and the width is remembered in that browser.
- **REQ_FE_023** — The panel has an X that hides it and gives the timeline the full width. Selecting any deliverable or milestone brings it back. Open or closed is remembered in that browser, on every screen that has the panel.
- **REQ_FE_024** — Clicking a milestone shows its name, date with its weekday ("Mar 12, 2026 (Thu)"), workstream, responsible person, status (the labelled badge with its icon), risk note (only when at risk), team, description (the rich text, nothing when it is empty) and links (under the description) in the panel, followed by read-only "Depends on" and "Blocks" lists as for a deliverable (REQ_FE_113). A read-only visitor sees all of it without any edit control; an admin also gets the Edit button in the panel header (REQ_FE_100) and Delete at the bottom (REQ_FE_090). The milestone panel carries no explanatory text about what can be done to milestones.
- **REQ_FE_090** — A milestone is created from the top bar's Milestone button (REQ_FE_101) (name, date, workstream; nothing is saved until "Create milestone" is clicked, the same explicit-save pattern as a deliverable) and deleted with the **Delete** button at the bottom of the panel's view mode, behind the single confirmation of REQ_FE_118, never silently — it never holds other content, so no move-target choice is needed the way a workstream delete has one. It is renamed, redated, described or moved to another workstream from the side panel (REQ_FE_100) or by dragging.
- **REQ_FE_025** — Every deliverable field can be edited in the edit window, and the panel's quick-edit form offers the same fields. The two never drift apart.
- **REQ_FE_026** — Editing saves by itself: a text field saves when you leave it (if the value is valid), and other controls save the moment you click them. There is no Save button and no "discard changes?" question. Each save sends only the one field that changed (a drag sends start and end together).
- **REQ_FE_027** — An invalid value shows its error right there, with a red border on the field, isn't saved, and keeps what you typed. Other fields that were valid stay saved.
- **REQ_FE_028** — Creating a new deliverable is different: it has an explicit "Create deliverable" button that saves everything at once.
- **REQ_FE_029** — A new deliverable starts with its team's default color, and the color picker has a "Reset to team color" action.
- **REQ_FE_030** — The "Move to another team" dialog — one dialog for deliverables and milestones — offers the other teams, matches workstreams by name with choices to create, pick or drop (a milestone: create or pick), shows a summary line, and shows a 3-year-limit problem with REQ_FE_062's message before you confirm.
- **REQ_FE_031** *(withdrawn — decommissioned 2026-10-11: there is no Undo after a move; the move is audited and can be made again by hand)*

- **REQ_FE_088** *(withdrawn — decommissioned 2026-10-11: see REQ_FE_002)*

## Status

- **REQ_FE_040** — A status badge sits on the deliverable's top-left corner and stays readable on any bar color. "At risk" adds a striped warning strip along the bottom of the bar. A milestone has no separate status icon: its diamond (22 px) shows the status itself at every zoom level *(Changed 2026-10-17: was a separate icon centred above a 15 px diamond)* — planned yellow with a black dashed outline *(Changed 2026-10-17: was a hollow diamond with a dashed grey outline)*, in progress the accent colour with a dot, done green with a check, at risk a plain red diamond with a white exclamation mark and a red outline around it, about 3 px clear of the diamond and 2 px thick *(Changed 2026-10-17: was a 24 px diamond with a striped edge in the hazard colours of the deliverable's warning strip)*; the outline reaches up to 6 px outside the diamond, so it takes part in the no-overlap rule (REQ_FE_010): it is narrowed, or left out, where the free stretch of the row has less room, and the name and date make room for it (no strip). The milestone's name is centred directly under the diamond, cut with an ellipsis to the free space of its row and left out where there is no room (it stays in the tooltip); its date, short and grey with the three-letter weekday in parentheses under it (e.g. "12 Oct" over "(Mon)"), both centred vertically on the diamond, is to the right of the diamond and left out where less than 40 px are free there.
- **REQ_FE_041** — Choosing "At risk" offers a short free-text reason, not required, shown on hover over the badge (a milestone: over its diamond) and in the panel when one was given.
- **REQ_FE_042** — *Hard rule.* The badge (and warning strip) shows on every deliverable's main block, and a milestone's diamond showing its own status (REQ_FE_040), on every roadmap screen, at every zoom level, and each of those screens shows the status legend.
- **REQ_FE_043** — *Hard rule.* The header row of every roadmap screen shows "N at risk" and "N in progress". They count deliverables and milestones together. On a team roadmap they count that team; on company and trace views they count every team. They update the moment a status changes.

## Copies and the company view

- **REQ_FE_050** — In its main workstream a deliverable is drawn solid; in every other workstream it belongs to it is drawn dashed and paler.
- **REQ_FE_051** — The team roadmap and the company view (not the trace) have a copies toggle in the top bar named after its action: "Hide multi-workstream" / "Show multi-workstream".
- **REQ_FE_052** — "See all combined roadmaps" (REQ_FE_123) opens the company view: every team's roadmap on one timeline whose rows are workstream names — same-named workstreams of all teams are one row. The row order is set on the Company order page (REQ_FE_094, 116). *(Changed 2026-10-11: the by-team view is gone.)*
- **REQ_FE_053** — The company view (not the trace) has a button `Show only team "<Team>" roadmap` at the left end of the zoom row (REQ_FE_066), for the team of the selected deliverable or milestone. It is not shown until something is selected; it leads to that team's roadmap `#/teams/<id>`.
- **REQ_FE_054** — *Hard rule.* On every screen that mixes teams (the company view, live and read-only, and the trace) each deliverable carries its team's short tag (ONB, PLT, SEC…) at its left edge, at every zoom level wherever the bar is wide enough to show it — currently width ≥ tag text + padding; a narrower bar carries its team in the hover text and the side panel instead. The name is shortened before the tag is ever hidden. *(Decision 2026-10-02: the one documented exception to the hard rule.)*

## The timeline

- **REQ_FE_060** — The visible range runs from the earliest to the latest date in the roadmap, and is shown (read-only) in the top bar.
- **REQ_FE_061** — An empty roadmap shows a window from 3 months before today to 9 months after.
- **REQ_FE_062** — *Hard rule.* Adding or editing something past the 3-year limit is blocked with the message "Maximum of 3 years of planning exceeded". Deleting, and edits that stay inside the current range, still work.
- **REQ_FE_063** — Dragging or editing a roadmap item past the end of the grid extends the grid right away to the end of that month — header, gridlines and scroll area together.
- **REQ_FE_064** — The header shows months above weeks. Week labels thin out as you zoom out, and every week cell shows its date range and ISO week number on hover.
- **REQ_FE_065** — One pair of functions, `dateToX` and `xToDate`, is the only place dates are turned into positions and back. Deliverables, milestones, arrows and gridlines all use it.
- **REQ_FE_066** — Zoom offers Year (the default), Half year, Quarter and Month. There is no "fit everything" — longer roadmaps are read by scrolling. The zoom choice is not in the top bar: it sits in its own row directly above the timeline, aligned right, as the word "Zoom" and a segmented Year | Half year | Quarter | Month, on the team roadmap, the company view and the trace. On the company view the team-roadmap button (REQ_FE_053) and then "Trace dependencies" (REQ_DEP_021) sit at the left end of that row, both shown only once a roadmap item is selected; there is no context bar under the top bar (REQ_FE_101). *(Decision 2026-10-15.)*
- **REQ_FE_067** — Long names are cut off with an ellipsis; the full name is available on hover, in the panel, or by zooming in.
- **REQ_FE_068** — Dragging snaps to the nearest day.
- **REQ_FE_069** — There is a horizontal scrollbar above the timeline as well as below it. They move together, and the top one lines up with the scrolling area.
- **REQ_FE_070** — The workstream names and both header rows stay in place while scrolling. A vertical marker and flag show today, and the past is lightly shaded — both from the browser's clock.
- **REQ_FE_071** — Zoom level, the copies and dependency toggles, and light/dark theme are each remembered in that browser, independently of one another and of the panel width.

- **REQ_FE_080** — There are exactly the 20 fixed icons from the spec. Only their labels can be changed, only by admins, for everyone at once. Anyone sees a label on hover.

- **REQ_FE_082** — All text meets WCAG AA contrast, including text on deliverables with user-chosen colors (the text color is computed from the background).

- **REQ_FE_084** — The layout holds at 1280px width. Up to 1439px button labels collapse to icons, and up to 1699px the date range and the switch labels shorten ("Show copies", "Show deps") — nothing reflows.
- **REQ_FE_085** — The explainer boxes that appear under some wireframes are not part of the real app.
